# 🕸️ Knowledge Graph Visualization - Simple Explanation

## What is a Knowledge Graph?

Think of a knowledge graph like a **smart map of information** that shows how different concepts, people, places, and ideas are connected to each other. Instead of just storing documents as separate files, we create a visual network that shows the relationships between different pieces of information.

## How Our System Works

### 1. **Document Processing** 📄
- When you upload documents (PDFs, Word files, etc.) to the system
- The system automatically reads and breaks down the content into smaller, manageable pieces
- It extracts key information like names, dates, locations, concepts, and topics

### 2. **Relationship Discovery** 🔍
- Our AI system (powered by Ollama 3.1) analyzes each piece of content
- It identifies how different pieces of information relate to each other
- For example: "John works at Company ABC" or "Meeting scheduled for Project XYZ"

### 3. **Graph Creation** 🕸️
- The system creates a visual network where:
  - **Nodes** (circles) = Individual pieces of information (people, companies, projects, etc.)
  - **Edges** (lines) = Relationships between those pieces
- Each organization (domain) gets its own separate graph to keep data secure

### 4. **Visual Display** 🎨
- The system generates a beautiful, interactive visualization
- Users can see the entire knowledge network at a glance
- Click "View Full Size" to see detailed connections
- Different colors and sizes help identify the most important information
